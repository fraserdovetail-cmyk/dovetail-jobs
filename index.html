How to use

  1. Fill in the form above with as much detail as you have.
  2. Click "Generate & save" — prompt is copied and saved to this job.
  3. Open claude.ai in a new chat.
  4. Upload the blank Excel template (Surname_-_scope_of_work.xlsx).
  5. Paste the prompt and send. Claude will populate all four tabs.
  6. Review the assumptions list before sending to the client.
